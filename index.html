In fact, it was the sector that received the most allocations consistently, since the 2000-01 Union budget.

What comes under finance sector spending?

▼

Spending includes the government's payments of interests on debts, which forms the largest chunk of spending within this sector, expenses on collecting and managing taxes, pensions for government employees, and departmental expenses like those of revenue, economic affairs, financial services and expenditure. These departments perform administrative functions related to maintaining accounts,
